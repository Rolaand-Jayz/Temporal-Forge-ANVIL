==================================================

Build the successor architecture correctly enough that a separate later campaign can rigorously determine whether it works.

==================================================
CODEC RULES
==================================================

Encoded codec motion vectors are side information, not truth.

For each supported codec, preserve as much as the API actually exposes:
- block/partition geometry;
- precision and units;
- reference identity/direction;
- temporal distance;
- frame type;
- skip/intra status;
- compound/bi-pred semantics where available.

Never fabricate missing fields.

Never run a new estimator and label its output "codec MVs."

Never assume VA-API/VCN MV export exists until proven on the actual stack.

Keep raw side information available for later research even if a densified/refined representation is also produced.
